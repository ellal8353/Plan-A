Phase guide

Menstrual (days 1–5): Low oestrogen and progesterone. Rest, warmth, iron-rich foods. Go gentle.

Follicular (days 6–13): Rising oestrogen. Energy builds. Good time for new challenges and harder training.

Ovulatory (days 14–16): Peak oestrogen. Highest energy and confidence. Best window for peak performance.

Luteal (days 17–28): Progesterone rises then falls. Energy dips toward end. Prioritise sleep and stress management.
